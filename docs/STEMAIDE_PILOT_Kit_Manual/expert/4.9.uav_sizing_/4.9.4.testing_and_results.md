# 4.9.4 Uav Sizing  Testing And Results

## Expected Result

| **Outcome** | **Success Criteria** |
| --- | --- |
| AUW calculated | Total all-up weight computed from component mass breakdown |
| Thrust calculated | Total hover thrust and per-motor thrust calculated with 1.5× safety factor |
| Power calculated | Motor power draw at cruise calculated; total system power summed |
| Battery sized | Battery capacity in mAh calculated from power draw and flight time |
| Motor selected | Motor specified: KV rating, maximum thrust, compatible ESC ampere rating |
| Propeller sized | Propeller diameter and pitch specified; matched to motor KV |
| Calculation report | All calculations shown; all formulas cited; results tabulated |

## Upgrade & Extension Ideas

* eCalc Cross-Check: enter your calculated values into ecalc.ch (free online UAV calculator) and compare results to your manual calculations
* Parametric Study: vary the flight time requirement from 15 to 60 minutes in 15-minute steps; graph battery capacity vs. flight time — this is a classic design trade-off
* Endurance vs. Payload Trade: keep AUW constant; graph how payload capacity changes as battery mass increases (endurance increases but payload decreases)
* Full Bill of Materials: research actual prices for every component at Accra electronics markets (Circle area) or Jumia Ghana; calculate total project cost
