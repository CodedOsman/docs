# 4.8.4 Uav Mission Requirements Testing And Results

## Expected Result

| **Outcome** | **Success Criteria** |
| --- | --- |
| Mission selected | One of four mission briefs chosen; 5 requirements quantified |
| 3 concepts generated | Each concept has a sketch, key features, and estimated weight |
| Decision matrix completed | All 7 criteria scored and weighted; totals calculated correctly |
| Best concept justified | Written justification explaining why the highest-scoring concept best meets the mission |
| Requirements report | 1-page report: mission brief, requirements, concepts, matrix, recommendation |
| Peer review | Another group reviews the requirements report and provides written feedback |


## Troubleshooting

## Upgrade & Extension Ideas

* Real Client Brief: approach a local farm, clinic, or government agency; present your requirements report as a real proposal; ask for their feedback
* Cost Estimate: add a cost column to the matrix; research component prices on local electronics markets in Accra; calculate total build cost for each concept
* Trade Study: perform a parametric trade study — vary payload from 100 g to 500 g in 100 g steps; graph the effect on required wing area and battery capacity
* Regulatory Risk Assessment: add a risk column — which concept has the highest regulatory risk in Ghana? Which is most likely to receive GCAA approval quickly?
