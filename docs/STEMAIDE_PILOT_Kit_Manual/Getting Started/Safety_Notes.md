# Safety Notes & Power Guidelines

This document contains important safety requirements and power guidelines for the STEMAIDE Pilot Kit projects.

## 1.1 Paper Dart Glider

| **⚠ Safety Requirements**  Power: None required – manual (hand) launch only.  Craft knives: Teacher or supervising adult must be present at all times during cutting.  Eye protection: Safety goggles are mandatory during all cutting activities.  Flight zone: Clear the area of all people before each launch; no one should stand in the flight path.  Supervision: Never allow unsupervised use of craft knives or cutting mats. |

## 1.10 Balsa Wood Tower

Load testing: Stand clear when adding final weights to avoid being hit by falling towers or weights. |

## 1.11 Straw Paper

| **⚠ Safety Notes**  Power: None required.  Scissors: Standard classroom supervision is sufficient.  Load testing: Stand clear when adding final weights — straw structures fail suddenly.  String: Ensure string used as a tie is not a trip hazard on the floor. |

## 1.12 Kite Building

| **⚠ Safety Notes**  Power: Wind only — no mechanical or electrical power.  Power lines: NEVER fly near overhead power lines. Choose a location with clear overhead space.  Road proximity: Kite flying must be in an open field away from roads — a falling kite can distract drivers.  String tension: Kite string under tension can cut skin. Wear gloves if available; do not wrap string around fingers.  Wind limit: Do not fly in gusts above 25 km/h — box kites especially can exert significant pull. |

## 1.14 Propeller Thrust

|  |
| --- |
| **⚠ Safety Requirements**  Power: 3V (2× AA batteries) — safe for students to handle.  Propellers: Safety goggles MANDATORY during all motor testing. A propeller shard at full speed is a serious eye hazard.  Propeller guard: Attach a wire cage around the propeller zone — propellers must never be at face height without a guard.  Rig stability: Clamp the base board before EVERY test run — thrust can cause the rig to move.  NEVER touch a spinning propeller. Wait until it has completely stopped before changing propellers. |

## 3.5 Flaps Spoilers

|  |
| --- |
| **⚠ Safety Requirements**  Power: LiPo battery – teacher connects and disconnects only.  First flight with flaps: set flaps to 15° only for the first landing; never deploy full flaps without practising partial first.  Pitch trim: flap deployment will cause a nose-up pitch — set a small down trim before deploying flaps.  Asymmetric flap: if one flap fails to extend, DO NOT fly — asymmetric flap causes severe roll.  Verify both flaps extend equally on the ground before every flight. |

## 4.6 Autonomous Formation

|  |
| --- |
| **⚠ CRITICAL – TWO DRONE SAFETY**  TWO drones in the air simultaneously doubles the risk. Both drones must be individually proven before formation flight.  Minimum separation during formation: 4 m. Never attempt a formation tighter than this with GPS-guided control.  Two designated spotters: one per drone; both call 'ABORT' if their drone behaves unexpectedly.  Teacher controls battery connections for BOTH drones.  Flight zone: minimum 50 m × 50 m; all observers at least 10 m outside the formation path.  Both drones land before any battery change or adjustment. |

## 4.8 Uav Mission Requirements

|  |
| --- |
| **Design Safety Requirements**  All UAV concepts must include as a minimum: Return to Launch (RTL) failsafe on signal loss; maximum operating altitude of 120 m above ground level (GCAA regulation); registration provision per GCAA Part 33.  Any concept intended for flight over people must include propeller guards, a geofence, and insurance provision.  These are not optional design features — they are regulatory requirements in Ghana. |

---

## 4.9 Uav Sizing

|  |
| --- |
| **Calculation Integrity**  Always include units in every calculation — a number without units is meaningless.  Double-check: thrust per motor must be at least 2× the motor's share of AUW weight at hover throttle.  If battery capacity exceeds 10,000 mAh: double-check all inputs — this is unusually large for a 1 kg UAV.  Battery safety: LiPo batteries sized at > 5,000 mAh require careful handling — reference Project 10 LiPo safety protocols. |
