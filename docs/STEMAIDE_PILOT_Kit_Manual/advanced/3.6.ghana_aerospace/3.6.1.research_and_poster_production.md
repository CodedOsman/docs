# 3.6.1 Ghana Aerospace Research And Poster Production

## Overview

Aviation is one of Ghana's fastest-growing industries — and one of its least understood career pathways. This project gives students deep, personal knowledge of a specific aviation career track: from the academic and licensing requirements, through the training journey and first job, to mid-career progression and salary expectations. Every career is researched in the Ghanaian context, grounded in GCAA requirements, and illustrated with at least one real Ghanaian professional as a role model.

## Step-by-Step Assembly

**Lesson 1 – Research & Poster Production**

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **STEP 1** | **Career Assignment** |
|  | * Assign one career track to each group of 3–4 students (see Career Reference Table, Section 5) * Career options: Commercial Pilot, ATCO, Aircraft Maintenance Engineer, Avionics Technician, Airport Operations, UAV Operator, Aerospace Engineer * Groups research 5 specific questions about their career: * 1. What does this person do every day? * 2. What qualifications and licences are required in Ghana? * 3. Which training institutions in Ghana or nearby offer this pathway? * 4. What is the approximate starting salary in Ghana? * 5. Who is one Ghanaian professional currently doing this job — and what is their story? |

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **STEP 2** | **Research Phase** |
|  | * Primary sources: GCAA official website (gcaa.com.gh), ICAO documentation, GACL website * Secondary sources: LinkedIn Ghana aviation professionals, Ghana Aviation News, Africa Aviation magazine * Each group must identify at least ONE named Ghanaian professional in their career track * Record all sources — no unsourced claims are accepted in the presentation * Teacher circulates and challenges vague facts: 'How do you know that? Can you show me the source?' |

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **STEP 3** | **Poster Design** |
|  | * Poster must include: career title, daily duties, GCAA licensing pathway (as a flowchart), training time, salary range, and the Ghanaian professional profile * Include at least one diagram or image: e.g. a cockpit, an ATC display, a maintenance logbook, a drone * Use the Career Reference Table (Section 5) as a starting point — verify and expand all information |

## Aviation Career Reference Table

| **Career Track** | **Licensing Body** | **Minimum Training** | **Typical Starting Role** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Commercial Pilot (CPL)** | GCAA Ghana / ICAO | 200 flight hours; CPL theory exams | First Officer, regional airline |
| **Air Traffic Controller (ATCO)** | GCAA Ghana | ATCO Basic + Rating course | ATC Assistant, approach control |
| **Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (AME)** | GCAA Ghana | B1/B2 training (2–3 years); exams | Licensed Apprentice, MRO |
| **Avionics Technician** | GCAA Ghana / City & Guilds | B2 avionics training; exams | Avionics Technician, airline |
| **Airport Operations Officer** | GCAA / Airport Authority | Diploma; security clearance | Ground Operations, GACL |
| **UAV / Drone Operator** | GCAA Ghana | Remote Pilot Certificate (RPC) | UAV Operator, survey company |
| **Aerospace Engineer** | GhIE / NaCCA | BSc Aerospace / Mechanical Engineering | Graduate Engineer, research |

|  |
| --- |
| **Aviation in Ghana – Key Institutions to Research**  Ghana Civil Aviation Authority (GCAA): gcaa.com.gh — the licensing body for all aviation professionals  Ghana Airports Company Limited (GACL): manages Kotoka International Airport and regional airports  Africa World Airlines (AWA): Ghana's largest domestic carrier; founded 2012  Passion Air: Ghanaian low-cost carrier; routes across West Africa  KNUST and UMaT: universities offering aerospace and engineering programmes in Ghana  GCAA Flight Academy, Accra: primary training centre for Ghanaian pilots and ATCOs |


## Presentation Assessment Rubric

| **Criterion** | **Excellent (4)** | **Good (3)** | **Developing (2)** | **Beginning (1)** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Career accuracy** | All facts verified; GCAA pathway correct | Most facts correct | Some errors | Significant inaccuracies |
| **Local relevance** | 2+ Ghanaian professionals named | 1 local example | Attempted | No local context |
| **Salary & training data** | Verified figures with source | Approximate figures | Estimated only | No data |
| **Presentation delivery** | Confident; all questions answered | Good; most questions answered | Acceptable | Unclear |
| **Visual quality** | Professional poster or slides | Good design | Acceptable | Difficult to read |
