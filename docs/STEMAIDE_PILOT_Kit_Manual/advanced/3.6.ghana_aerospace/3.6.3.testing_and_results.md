# 3.6.3 Ghana Aerospace Testing And Results

## Expected Result

| **Outcome** | **Success Criteria** |
| --- | --- |
| Career profile researched | GCAA licensing pathway; training institutions; minimum requirements all documented |
| Ghanaian professional identified | At least one named Ghanaian currently working in the chosen career track |
| Salary range documented | Verified approximate starting and mid-career salary for Ghana |
| Presentation delivered | 5-minute presentation to class; questions answered confidently |
| Rubric score | Total score of 12 or higher out of 16 on the presentation rubric |
| Class career map produced | Group activity: all 7 career tracks mapped on a single poster |


## Troubleshooting
For common errors and troubleshooting, please refer to [Troubleshooting](../../Appendices/Troubleshooting.md#3-6-ghana-aerospace).

## Upgrade & Extension Ideas

* Guest Speaker: invite a Ghanaian pilot, ATCO, or AME to speak to the class — contact GCAA or AWA HR departments
* Airport Visit: arrange a guided visit to Kotoka International Airport operations area — contact the GACL Education Officer
* Salary Database: the class collectively creates an Aviation Careers Salary Database for Ghana; update it annually
* Career Pathway Video: record a 2-minute video interview with each group presenting their career; compile into a class documentary
