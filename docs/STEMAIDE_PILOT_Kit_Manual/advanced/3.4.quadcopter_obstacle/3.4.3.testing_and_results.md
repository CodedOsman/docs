## Expected Result

| **Outcome** | **Success Criteria** |
| --- | --- |
| Course completed | All gates and slalom elements navigated in correct sequence |
| Time recorded | At least one clean timed run with a recorded time |
| Under 90 seconds | Best total time (including penalties) under 90 seconds |
| Landing in zone | At least one landing within the 1 m diameter zone |
| No crashes | No tip-overs, propeller strikes, or structural damage during scored runs |
| Timing sheet | All 3 runs recorded with raw time and penalties |

## Upgrade & Extension Ideas

* Multi-Lap Course – Fly the course twice in succession without landing; total time for both laps
* Team Relay – Two pilots; pilot 1 flies Gates A and B; hands to pilot 2 for Gate C, slalom, and landing
* Night Course – Fit LEDs to the gate frames; fly the course under low-light conditions
* FPV Course – All timed runs flown using FPV goggles (Project 15 prerequisite)
* Payload Race – Carry the Project 17 cargo hook payload through the course; release it in the landing zone
