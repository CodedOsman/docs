## Expected Result

| **Outcome** | **Success Criteria** |
| --- | --- |
| Maintenance schedule created | All 5 maintenance types represented; task references assigned; intervals correct |
| 8 tasks performed and signed | All 8 tasks in the task table completed; each signed by performing student and countersigned by teacher |
| Maintenance log complete | All 8 tasks logged with date, description, parts, hours, and signature |
| Defect found and documented | At least one actual defect found and correctly documented with recommended action |
| CAMO framework explained | Student explains the role of a CAMO in managing an aircraft's continuing airworthiness |
| Regulations identified | Student identifies the GCAA regulation and ICAO Annex that govern UAV maintenance |

## Upgrade & Extension Ideas

* Digital Maintenance Log: recreate the logbook as a Google Sheet or simple database; practice digital record-keeping
* Time Between Overhaul (TBO): research what TBO means for aircraft engines; calculate the TBO for a Lycoming O-320 engine vs. the ESC in this kit
* Maintenance Schedule Software: use a free maintenance management app (e.g. Flightdocs free tier) to schedule and track the 8 tasks
* Real AME Interview: invite a licensed GCAA AME to class; ask about a real maintenance decision they had to make
