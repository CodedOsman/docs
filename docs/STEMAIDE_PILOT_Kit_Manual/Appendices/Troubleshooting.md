# Common Errors & Troubleshooting

Use this guide to diagnose and fix common issues encountered during the Pilot Kit projects.

## 1.1 Paper Dart Glider

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Aircraft turns left/right** | Asymmetric folding or wing shape | Refold carefully; check for symmetry |
| **Aircraft nosedives** | Too much nose weight | Add tail weight or adjust leading-edge folds |
| **Aircraft stalls** | Too much tail weight | Add nose weight or adjust folds |
| **Short flight distance** | Drag from poor folds | Smooth all folds; ensure clean flat surfaces |
| **Inconsistent data** | Different launch angles or force | Standardise launch method across group |

## 1.10 Balsa Wood Tower

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tower leans to one side** | Joints not square during assembly | Use a right-angle card at every joint; hold for 60 s while glue sets |
| **Joints fail before columns** | Insufficient glue or not fully cured | Apply glue generously; allow 20 min cure before adding more weight |
| **Columns buckle under low load** | No cross-bracing on vertical members | Add diagonal braces between columns; triangulate all panel faces |
| **Tower too heavy to be efficient** | Too much material used | Use the thinnest balsa strips that can still carry the load; plan before building |

---

## 1.11 Straw Paper

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Too weak — collapses under first weight** | No cross-bracing in fuselage | Add diagonal straw braces between fuselage members |
| **Joints fail at tape** | Tape applied over oil or dust | Clean straw surfaces with dry cloth; press tape firmly for 10 s |
| **Fuselage bends instead of breaks** | No spine spar | Add a single long straw along the centreline as the main spar |
| **Wing deflects too much** | No spanwise spar | Add a straw along the leading edge to resist bending |

---

## 1.12 Kite Building

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kite will not launch** | Too heavy or insufficient wind | Remove excess tape; wait for stronger wind (10+ km/h) |
| **Spins and dives** | No tail or tail too short | Add a 60 cm tail; increase tail length until stable |
| **Nose dives repeatedly** | Bridle angle too flat | Move the bridle attachment point upward to increase the angle of attack |
| **Kite drifts sideways** | Asymmetric frame or cover | Check both sides of the frame are identical; re-centre the cover |

## 1.13 Airfoil Shape Comparison

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No lift measured** | Fan too far from airfoil | Move fan to within 20 cm of the airfoil leading edge |
| **Inconsistent readings** | Fan speed varies between tests | Mark the fan speed dial and return to same position each test; allow 5 s to stabilise |
| **Airfoil twists under airload** | Mount too flexible | Reinforce the mounting rod; use a stiffer cardboard backing |
| **Scale reads negative (downforce)** | Airfoil mounted inverted | Check cambered surface is facing upward into the airstream |
| **No stall observed** | Angle not increased enough | Continue increasing to 20°, 25°; stall is usually between 15° and 20° |

## 1.14 Propeller Thrust

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Motor does not spin** | Wrong wiring or open circuit | Check all connections; confirm battery polarity; test with a multimeter |
| **Rig moves during testing** | Not clamped to table | Clamp the base board to the table with a G-clamp; retest |
| **Inconsistent thrust readings** | Propeller unbalanced | Balance propeller using a prop balancer; sand heavier blade lightly |
| **Low thrust on all propellers** | Motor direction reversed | Swap any two motor wires; confirm propeller pushes air rearward |
| **Spring scale bounces** | Vibration from unbalanced propeller | Increase balancing; read scale at peak of oscillation average |

## 1.2 Foam Hand Launch

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nose dives** | CG too far forward / nose-heavy | Add tail weight or remove nose weight; adjust elevator up slightly |
| **Stalls (climbs then drops)** | CG too far back / tail-heavy | Add nose weight or remove tail weight; adjust elevator down slightly |
| **Turns left or right** | Asymmetric wing or rudder misaligned | Check wing symmetry; gently bend rudder opposite to the turn |
| **Unexpected loop** | Elevator trimmed too high | Bend elevator trailing edge down by 1–2 mm |
| **Short glide distance** | Rough foam edges causing drag | Re-sand all edges; ensure wing surfaces are flat and smooth |
| **Wing detaches** | Insufficient glue at joint | Re-glue with hot glue; allow full cure time before flight |

## 1.3 Rubber Band Powered

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Propeller does not spin** | Rubber band looped in wrong direction | Re-loop rubber band; confirm it twists to spin the propeller forward |
| **Rubber band snaps** | Too many turns or degraded rubber | Reduce turns by 25; replace rubber band; keep spare bands available |
| **Aircraft turns immediately** | Asymmetric wing or misaligned fin | Check wing symmetry by sighting from nose; bend rudder slightly opposite |
| **Very short flight time** | Aircraft too heavy | Check all joints for excess glue; reduce number of ribs if needed |
| **Nose dives on launch** | CG too far forward | Move wing 5 mm toward tail; re-check CG |
| **Propeller wobbles** | Loose propeller hub | Tighten hub screw; add small washer as bearing |

## 1.4 Rc Flight Simulator

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Aircraft stalls on takeoff** | Too much up elevator applied too early | Apply elevator gently after reaching takeoff speed; increase throttle first |
| **Crashes immediately after launch** | Incorrect control direction input | Practice stick movements with aircraft stationary; confirm Mode 2 setup |
| **Cannot maintain altitude** | Throttle not held steady | Set throttle to ~60%; make small corrections rather than large inputs |
| **Overcontrolling (oscillates)** | Stick inputs too large | Make small, smooth inputs; wait for the aircraft to respond before correcting |
| **Landing too fast** | Throttle too high on final approach | Begin throttle reduction on base leg; aim for a slow, stable approach speed |
| **Aircraft rolls on takeoff** | Aileron or rudder not centred | Re-calibrate controller; centre trims before each flight |

## 1.5 Pre Flight Safety Checklist

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Defects missed during inspection** | Inspection rushed; not systematic | Slow down; follow 10-point sequence strictly; verbalise each check aloud |
| **Checklist submitted unsigned** | Sign-off step overlooked | Always complete sign-off block before submitting; teacher checks before accepting |
| **Safety rules forgotten** | Insufficient review | Read safety poster aloud at start of each lesson; quiz each other in pairs |
| **Inspection sequence inconsistent** | Different order each time | Always follow the printed 10-point sequence; practice with a partner timing you |
| **Defects not described clearly** | Vague notes | Record exact location and nature: e.g. 'crack at left wing root, 2 cm long' |

## 1.6 Aircraft Classification

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wrong category assignment** | Confusion between powered lift and fixed-wing | Review the lift source: fixed-wing uses aerofoils only; powered lift uses rotors or jets for VTOL |
| **Spacecraft included in 'aircraft'** | Misconception that any flying vehicle is an aircraft | Clarify definition: aircraft operate within Earth's atmosphere; spacecraft exit it |
| **Silhouettes hard to identify** | Too small or poorly drawn | Use a ruler and pencil first; trace or use printed templates; scale to fill the space |
| **Missing Ghana context** | Local aviation not researched | Direct students to GCAA website and Africa World Airlines fleet page for local examples |
| **Poster unbalanced / crowded** | Poor layout planning | Use pencil grid first; allocate equal space per category before starting in pen |

## 1.7 Four Forces Demonstration

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Streamers barely move** | Fan too far away or speed too low | Move airfoil closer; increase fan speed |
| **No visible lift difference between airfoils** | Airfoil not oriented correctly | Ensure cambered airfoil curved side faces upward into airstream |
| **Force diagram arrows reversed** | Confusion over direction conventions | Review: Lift=up, Weight=down, Thrust=forward, Drag=backward |
| **Turbulence at 0° on cambered foil** | Airfoil inverted | Flip so curved surface faces the fan outlet |

## 1.8 Centre Of Gravity

## 2.10 Jet Engine Principles

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rocket will not launch** | Pump pressure too low or seal leaking | Increase pump strokes to 80+; check rubber stopper seal; ensure stopper fits tightly |
| **Rocket tumbles in flight** | Fins asymmetric or CG behind CP | Check fin alignment; add clay to nose to move CG forward |
| **Very short flight** | Too little water (< 25%) | Try 30–40% water fill; this range usually gives best performance |
| **Water sprays sideways** | Stopper not centred in bottle neck | Re-centre stopper; ensure launch rod guides the bottle vertically |
| **Fins fall off during flight** | Insufficient glue on fin roots | Apply glue generously; allow full cure time; tape over the root junction |

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ICAO and GCAA confused** | Both described as making rules | Clarify: ICAO sets international standards; GCAA enforces them within Ghana — GCAA is the national implementation |
| **Annex content too vague** | Research from unreliable sources | Direct to ICAO.int for official Annex summaries; GCAA.com.gh for Ghana-specific regulations |

| **Facts unverified** | Internet sources not cross-checked | Require all facts to be referenced; ICAO and GCAA official websites are the only accepted primary sources |
| **No Ghana examples** | Research only looked at global level | Ask: how does this rule affect a pilot flying from Accra to Kumasi? This focuses research locally |

## 2.12 Water Bottle

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rocket barely leaves the rod** | Pressure too low or seal leaking | Pump to 80 PSI; press stopper firmly; replace stopper if seal is poor |
| **Rocket spins in flight** | Fins not symmetrical; one fin larger | Check all four fins are identical; re-tape any that are loose or angled |
| **Very low height with 50% water** | Too much water; not enough air volume | Optimal fill is 25–35%; water above this reduces the compressed air volume |
| **Rocket drifts downwind** | No correction for wind | Aim slightly into the wind; adjust launch angle toward the wind direction |
| **Data table inconsistent** | Different launchers apply different pressure | Standardise: same launcher every flight; same pump stroke count; use a pressure gauge |

## 2.13 Gyroscope Accelerometer

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **All axes read 0** | I2C address wrong or wiring fault | Run I2C scanner; confirm 0x68 detected; check SDA GPIO 21 and SCL GPIO 22 |
| **Pitch/roll very noisy** | No filter applied | Apply complementary filter: angle = 0.98×(gyro) + 0.02×(accel) |
| **Gyro drifts after 30 seconds** | Normal gyroscope integration error | This demonstrates WHY the complementary filter is needed — show before and after |
| **Heading Indicator drifts to wrong value** | No magnetometer; gyro integration only | Explain: a real heading indicator uses a gyro + compass; this demo uses gyro only — drift is expected |
| **Serial Plotter shows flat line** | Wrong variable sent to Serial.println | Ensure pitch, roll, and yaw\_rate are on separate Serial.print lines separated by commas |

## 2.2 Build A Tethered

| **No transmitter response** | Receiver not bound or failsafe triggered | Rebind: hold bind button while powering receiver; check failsafe settings |
| **Drone drifts sideways** | IMU not calibrated | Calibrate IMU in Betaflight with drone on a level surface |

## 2.5 Cockpit Instrument Panel

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GPS no fix indoors** | Satellite signal blocked | Take unit outdoors; allow 60–90 s for cold start fix |
| **Compass heading incorrect** | Magnetic interference from nearby electronics | Calibrate with figure-8 waving motion; keep compass 5 cm from other modules |
| **TFT display blank** | Wrong SPI pin assignment or incorrect CS/DC wiring | Re-check SPI pins against wiring table; confirm CS and DC are not swapped |
| **I2C sensor not detected** | Address conflict or wiring error | Run I2C scanner; verify SDA/SCL; check for address jumper on BMP280 |
| **Altitude drifts with temperature** | BMP280 requires temperature compensation | Enable oversampling and IIR filter in BMP280 library settings |
| **Attitude drifts over time** | Gyroscope accumulates error (gyro drift) | Implement complementary filter: fuse gyroscope with accelerometer data |
| **GPS speed always 0** | No movement or fix not 3D | Move the unit outdoors; wait for 3D fix (needs 4+ satellites) |

## 2.6 Quadcopter Manual

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Misses waypoints consistently** | Poor heading awareness; over-relying on visual | Use landmarks or heading indicator; practise single-leg flights before full mission |
| **Unstable hover at waypoint** | Overcorrecting stick inputs | Make smaller corrections; wait 0.5 s before correcting again; altitude hold mode if available |
| **Altitude fluctuates in transit** | Inconsistent throttle during movement | Set a base throttle for level flight; only adjust slightly for altitude corrections |
| **Drifts off leg heading** | Wind or IMU drift | Apply small roll/pitch correction into wind; re-check heading at each waypoint |
| **Hard landing** | Throttle cut too quickly on descent | Reduce throttle in small steps; final descent rate should be 0.5 m/s or less |
| **Battery depleted before mission ends** | High power consumption from corrections | Practice more; reduce unnecessary corrections; use a fully charged battery |

## 2.7 Led Navigation

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LED does not illuminate** | Anode/cathode reversed or GPIO wrong | Flip LED; confirm long leg to GPIO via resistor |
| **LED too dim** | Resistor value too high | Reduce to 330Ω; measure LED brightness in a darkened room |
| **Strobe rate wrong** | Delay value incorrect in code | Adjust delay\_ms value: 750 ms on + 750 ms off = 40 flashes/min |
| **Landing light always on** | Wrong logic in CH6 code | Check channel 6 PWM value threshold; reverse if/else condition |
| **LEDs draw too much current** | No resistors in circuit | Never connect LEDs directly to GPIO without resistors — GPIO will burn out |

## 2.8 Altitude Climb Rate

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Altitude drifts indoors** | Pressure changes from doors/windows/people moving | Normal — BMP280 is sensitive; calibrate outdoors in stable air before flight |
| **SD card not recognised** | Card not formatted FAT32 or wrong SPI pin | Format card as FAT32 in Windows/Mac; verify CS pin assignment in code |
| **Altitude noisy (jumps ±2 m)** | No IIR filter applied | Enable oversampling (OSS=4) and IIR filter (coeff=4) in BMP280 library config |
| **VSI always zero** | Time step not measured correctly | Use millis() to measure exact time between readings; divide altitude change by elapsed ms |
| **CSV file corrupted** | SD card removed before file closed | Add card.close() in the landing detection code; always power off before removing SD |

## 2.9 Aviation Weather

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **DHT22 reads 0 or -999** | Wrong GPIO or 10kΩ pull-up resistor missing | Add 10kΩ resistor between data pin and VCC; confirm correct GPIO |
| **Pressure drifts after power-on** | BMP280 needs warm-up time | Wait 2 minutes after power-on before recording; allow thermal stabilisation |
| **Anemometer reads 0 in wind** | Hall effect sensor wiring issue | Confirm magnet passes the sensor; check VCC and GND; test with multimeter |
| **Data disappears on power-off** | No SD card or logging not enabled | Add MicroSD module; log to file; alternatively use Serial to PC |
| **Readings vary wildly** | Station in direct sunlight | Mount station in shade or use a radiation shield over temperature sensor |

## 3.13 Flight Data Recorder

## 3.4 Quadcopter Obstacle

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Consistently hits gates** | Flying too fast for skill level; poor approach line | Slow down significantly; practise each gate individually first |
| **Misses turns between gates** | Not planning the route ahead | Look at the next gate before reaching the current one |
| **Loses altitude in slalom** | Over-concentrating on lateral movement; forgetting throttle | Set a base throttle before entering slalom; make only lateral corrections |
| **Hard landing** | Descending too fast on landing approach | Reduce throttle earlier; approach at a shallow angle; flare before touchdown |
| **Over-controls after gates** | Correction inputs too large | Make smaller inputs; accept slight imprecision; smooth > precise |
| **Crashes on first run** | Attempting full speed before skills are ready | Walk the course; fly each element slowly twice before timed run |

## 3.5 Flaps Spoilers

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Flap deflects wrong direction** | Servo arm on wrong side of hinge | Move servo arm to other side; or reverse channel in transmitter |
| **Asymmetric flap extension** | Pushrod lengths unequal on left and right | Trim both pushrods to identical length; verify equal deflection angle |
| **Aircraft pitches nose-up with flaps** | Normal — flaps shift lift rearward | Add slight down elevator trim when flaps are extended |
| **Spoiler causes hard roll** | Spoiler deployed fully on approach — too much drag asymmetry | Deploy spoiler symmetrically on both wings; ensure both panels open equally |
| **Servo stalls (buzzes)** | Hinge friction too high | Sand hinge; add a small amount of petroleum jelly to the hinge line |

## 3.7 Aircraft Materials

| **Spring scale reading fluctuates** | Material stretching gradually | Wait 10 s before reading; take average of 3 readings |
| **Balsa fails before test starts** | Grain direction wrong | Cut balsa along the grain for tensile tests; use cross-grain only for shear tests |
| **Fibreglass cloth hard to grip** | Slippery surface | Fold a 2 cm tab at each end and clamp securely; use sandpaper on the grip area |
| **Foam compresses instead of failing** | EVA foam is highly deformable | Note the deformation rather than failure — foam is not a structural material; this is expected |
| **Deflection hard to measure accurately** | Eye-level variation | Set up the beam at table edge; use a ruler clamped vertically beneath the deflection point |

## 3.8 Moments Stability

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pitching moment calculated wrong** | Force and moment arm not perpendicular | Draw a diagram first; confirm the arm is measured perpendicular to the force vector |
| **Confused roll and yaw** | Names learned without physical reference | Point to each axis on the Project 27 demonstrator while naming it; physical beats verbal |
| **Balance beam does not match calculation** | Different scale of forces | This is expected — verify the ratio, not the absolute value |
| **Three-view drawing inaccurate** | Orthographic projection not understood | Show a worked example of a box in three views before attempting the aircraft |

## 3.9 Vhf Radio Communication

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Using casual language** | No exposure to ICAO phraseology | Print the phraseology card; insist on ICAO format; re-read the transmission correctly |

## 4.1 Design Build A Custom

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Frame too flexible** | Material too thin or joints not reinforced | Add gussets at arm joints; use thicker plate; consider carbon strip reinforcement |
| **Motors mismatched to propellers** | Wrong KV for selected prop size | Use motor KV calculator: KV × battery voltage = unloaded RPM; check prop chart |

| **Short flight time** | Battery too small or motors drawing too much current | Measure actual current draw with a watt meter; select battery accordingly |

## 4.3 Servo Controlled

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Servo does not move** | Wrong wiring: signal, power, or ground swapped | Check servo lead: orange/yellow = signal; red = power; brown/black = ground |
| **Control surface binds (won't move fully)** | Hinge too tight or pushrod misaligned | Loosen hinge tape; realign pushrod so it pulls in a straight line |

| **Movement in wrong direction** | Servo arm on wrong side | Move servo arm to opposite side; or reverse servo direction in transmitter |
| **Servo jitters constantly** | Low battery voltage | Replace or recharge the 4.8V battery |

## 4.4 Rc Plane Wing

| --- | --- | --- |
| **CG shifts with added weight** | Weights not placed at CG point | Tape all weights at the marked CG position; re-check balance before each flight |
| **Stall speed not identifiable** | Aircraft too fast to observe stall onset | Reduce throttle gradually during level flight; note the speed at which the aircraft begins to mush |

## 4.5 Vtol Hybrid

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |

| **Unstable hover** | FC PID not tuned for VTOL hover mode | Tune hover PID in QGroundControl or Mission Planner; reduce I gain first |
| **Transition failure — nose pitches** | Transition speed too low; wing not generating enough lift | Increase forward speed before tilting; adjust transition airspeed parameter |

## 4.6 Autonomous Formation

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Communication lost** | Range exceeded or RF interference | Reduce inter-drone distance; switch to a less crowded frequency |
| **Wingman position offset too large** | GPS accuracy limit; latency | Reduce flight speed; increase waypoint tolerance in ArduPilot |

## 4.7 Long Endurance

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No solar charge** | Panel wiring error or MPPT not connected | Check polarity; confirm MPPT IN+ goes to panel positive; use multimeter to verify current |
| **Battery depletes during flight** | Motor power exceeds solar input | Reduce throttle; fly in faster thermals; increase panel area; reduce aircraft weight |
| **Aircraft too heavy to fly** | Solar panels overweight | Use flexible thin-film panels; reduce battery capacity; lighten airframe |
| **MPPT gets very hot** | MPPT undersized for panel | Select MPPT rated at 1.5× panel peak current |
| **Motor weak even on full battery** | Motor KV too high for large propeller | Switch to lower KV motor; or use smaller propeller |

| **Requirements too vague** | Mission not defined with numbers | Every requirement must have a specific number: 'heavy' → '300 g payload' |

## 4.9 Uav Sizing

| **Error** | **Likely Cause** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Battery capacity seems impossibly large** | Motor efficiency too low or flight time too long | Check η\_motor = 0.75–0.85 for good motors; verify flight time is realistic |
| **Thrust per motor seems very high** | AUW overestimated | Weigh actual components; a typical 1 kg quadcopter needs only 3–4 N per motor |
| **Wing area result is tiny** | Wrong units — m vs cm | Confirm all inputs in SI units: AUW in kg, velocity in m/s, area in m² |
| **Propeller selection unclear** | KV × voltage = RPM relationship not applied | Use: RPM = KV × battery voltage (e.g. 900 KV × 11.1V = 9990 RPM); select prop to match |
| **Different groups get very different answers** | Different assumptions | Standardise: all groups use ρ = 1.2 kg/m³, η\_motor = 0.8, CL = 1.2, safety factor = 1.5 |
