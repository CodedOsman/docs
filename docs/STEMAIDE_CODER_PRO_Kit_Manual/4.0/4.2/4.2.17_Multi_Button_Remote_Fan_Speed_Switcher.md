## Mounting the component on the breadboard and wiring the circuit

**Step 1:** Connect the IR Receiver Signal pin to Arduino Pin 11, VCC to 5V, and GND to GND.

**Step 2:** Connect the L298N Motor Driver ENA to Pin 9, IN1 to Pin 8, and IN2 to Pin 7. Connect motor leads to the driver output terminals.

**Step 3:** Connect the IIC 1602 LCD SDA pin to Arduino Pin A4 and the SCL pin to Pin A5. Connect VCC to 5V and GND to GND.

**Step 4:** Verify all GND connections are common across the Arduino, Motor Driver, and LCD.

**Step 5:** Double-check all VCC/GND connections to prevent electrical shorts.

**Step 6:** Connect the USB cable only after verifying all wiring.
